## Better-Fit Environments

- Labs with clear experiment infrastructure and reproducibility norms.
- Advisors who provide periodic feedback without micromanaging every step.
- Projects where failure analysis, ablation, and iteration are valued.

## Less-Fit Environments

- Extremely hands-off supervision with no feedback rhythm.
- Labs that reward only positive results and do not document failed experiments.
- Projects where research questions change weekly without cumulative learning.
